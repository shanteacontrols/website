The large modular synthesizer is a MIDI controller that controls the 'Arturia Modular V3' software synth. 4-off OpenDeck boards are used in the two top cabinets and other MIDI controller boards for the rest of the system including the 49 key keyboard.

The other two synths are a MK1 (Black front panel) MIDI controller built into a 'Minimoog Model D' Walnut case, using a single OpenDeck controller board, one other controller plus the keyboard. The other synth, a MK2 version (Blue front panel), uses a single OpenDeck controller board plus the keyboard. Both instruments are designed to work with the 'Arturia Mini V3' software synth. The other Walnut case was made by myself.

Although I am more of an Engineer than a musician, I do enjoy experimenting with electronic sounds and these instruments have been a pleasure to use over recent years and have been very reliable.

All the above projects have been made a whole lot easier using your OpenDeck controller boards and I thank you for that.
