Control with movement,
touch, and light.

Build an instrument you play without touching, visuals that follow your movement, or a scene that responds to light. Configure the sensor in OpenDeck, stream its data over Ethernet OSC, and shape the response in the software of your choice, including Processing, TouchDesigner, QLab, and many more.

  1. 01

    Choose your hardware

    Pick the connections and I/O you need, then follow your board’s wiring guide for buttons, encoders, pots, LEDs, or sensors.

  2. 02

    Install OpenDeck

    Flash a supported development board, or start with an official OpenDeck board. Installation and update options depend on the target.

  3. 03

    Configure in the browser

    Set mappings and device behavior, then connect to your music, visuals, or control software. No firmware coding required.
