Control with movement,
touch, and light.

Build an instrument you play without touching, visuals that follow your movement, or a scene that responds to light. Configure the sensor in OpenDeck, stream its data over Ethernet OSC, and shape the response in the software of your choice, including Processing, TouchDesigner, QLab, and many more.

APDS-9960 · Ambient light

Light becomes part of the scene.

Cover or expose the sensor to dim and brighten a virtual room, open its blinds, and change the motion of floating dust.

Try this example ↗

APDS-9960 · Proximity

Make visuals respond to presence.

Move a hand toward the sensor to grow a glowing bloom, brighten its core, and energize the particles around it.

Try this example ↗

BNO085 · Orientation

Turn movement into a controller.

Rotate and tilt the sensor to steer a 3D cube. Orientation data connects physical movement to the scene on screen.

Try this example ↗

VL53L5CX · Depth sensing

Explore interaction in three dimensions.

Move your hand above an 8×8 distance sensor and see its shape as a live point cloud. Use the same depth data for position, presence, or touchless instruments.

Try this example ↗

Pick a board. Make it your own.

  1. 01

    Choose your hardware

    Pick the connections and I/O you need, then follow your board’s wiring guide for buttons, encoders, pots, LEDs, or sensors.

  2. 02

    Install OpenDeck

    Flash a supported development board, or start with an official OpenDeck board. Installation and update options depend on the target.

  3. 03

    Configure in the browser

    Set mappings and device behavior, then connect to your music, visuals, or control software. No firmware coding required.
